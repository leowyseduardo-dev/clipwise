Frequently asked questions

How does Clipwise work?

Paste a YouTube, Kick or Twitch link or upload a video. The engine downloads it when needed, transcribes, detects scenes and scores moments, then renders vertical clips with subtitles.

How are clips counted?

Each successfully generated clip consumes one unit. Failed processing does not consume a unit.

What happens when I run out of credits?

You'll see a message with upgrade options. Daily limits reset every day, monthly limits per billing cycle.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, anytime. You keep access until the end of the paid period.

Is my video data safe?

Videos are processed per user and access is protected by authentication.
