1. What Clipwise is

Clipwise is a desktop application that helps creators edit and publish their own video content. It analyzes videos stored locally on the user's computer, generates short-form vertical clips with automatic subtitles, and can upload the resulting clips to the user's own YouTube and TikTok accounts through the official APIs of those platforms.
