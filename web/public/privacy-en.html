Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Summary

Clipwise is a desktop application that runs entirely on your computer. We do not operate any server that receives your videos, your credentials or your published content. There is no account registration with us, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

2. Data Clipwise stores, and where

DataWhere it livesWhy
Videos you import and the clips generated from themLocal application data folder on your computerTo edit and render your clips
OAuth access & refresh tokens for the YouTube / TikTok accounts you connectLocal SQLite database on your computerTo publish to your own channels on your behalf, only when you click publish
App settings (subtitle style, privacy defaults, clip count)Local configuration file on your computerTo remember your preferences

3. What leaves your computer

Only two things ever leave your computer, and both are actions you trigger yourself:

  • Video uploads. When you click publish, the generated clip is uploaded directly to YouTube via the YouTube Data API or to TikTok via the Content Posting API.
  • OAuth authorization. When you connect an account, your browser opens the platform's own login/consent page. Clipwise never sees your platform password — it receives only the access token that the platform issues.

4. What we share

Nothing. We do not sell, rent, transfer or have access to any of your data. Clipwise does not include third-party analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs.

5. Token revocation and deletion

You can disconnect a connected account at any time from the publishing settings in the app, which deletes the stored tokens from your local database. You can also revoke Clipwise's access from your Google Security settings (Third-party access) or your TikTok account settings (Apps and permissions). Deleting the application folder removes all locally stored data.

6. Children

Clipwise is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any data from them — no data is collected from any user at all.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
